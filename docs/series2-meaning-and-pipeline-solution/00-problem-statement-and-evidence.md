## 2. Root-Cause Diagnosis: Why the Final Videos (`Master_Part1..5_Fruits_of_Power_Farsi.mp4`) Lost Their Narrative Meaning

Inspecting the evidence in `docs/series2-meaning-and-pipeline-solution/contact_sheets/`:
- `original_16scene_part1.jpg` .. `original_16scene_part5.jpg` (the 16-scene contact sheets of the original 65–75s `straw.berryjammin` source videos)
- `extracted_6kfs_part1.jpg` .. `extracted_6kfs_part5.jpg` (the 6 keyframes extracted per episode by `series2_scripts/05_extract_clean_episode_kfs.py`)
- `flow_6clip_master_part1.jpg` .. `flow_6clip_master_part5.jpg` (the actual rendered `Master_FruitsOfPower_Part1..5_Flow_Native_Farsi.mp4` contact sheets)
- `parts1_to_5_clips_probe.json` (the quantitative L1 keyframe distance & internal scene-cut probe across all 30 clips in Parts 1–5)

reveals **4 systemic architectural bugs** in the current pipeline:

### Bug 1: 70% of Visual Story Beats Were Discarded by Compressing 16–20 Fast Cuts Into Only 6 Static 10-Second Clips
- Each original `straw.berryjammin` episode (`65s–77s`) has **16 to 22 fast cinematic cuts (every 3–4 seconds)** that establish **visual cause and effect** (silent reaction shots, phone screen close-ups, kiss shots, DNA envelope opening, ultrasound monitor, mansion attackers).
- `05_extract_clean_episode_kfs.py` sampled **only 6 timestamps per episode** and tried to replace the entire 70-second 18-scene movie with **6 static 10-second talking-head clips**:
  - **Part 1 (`original_16scene_part1.jpg` vs `flow_6clip_master_part1.jpg`)**:
    - In the original (`original_16scene_part1.jpg`), Duke screams in the pool (`2.1s`), Lemonella & Banana Man kiss on the yellow sports car (`10.4s`), Duke checks his phone (`14.6s`: *"Last night was wild 🍌"*), walks in with purple roses and drops them (`18.7s–22.9s`) because he sees them kissing (`27.1s–31.2s`), and watches them kiss by the fountain from the balcony (`43.7s–47.9s`).
    - In the 6-clip Flow master (`flow_6clip_master_part1.jpg`), all bridge shots are gone, and each character speaks for ~3s and then stares blankly for ~7s of dead air.

### Bug 2: Silent Fallback to 0-Ingredient Text-to-Video (`KEYFRAME_MISMATCH`) & Leftover Composer Chips (`MULTI_INGREDIENT_CONTAMINATION`)
- As proven by `parts1_to_5_clips_probe.json`, out of 30 clips across Parts 1–5, **12 clips failed keyframe lock (`l1_vs_keyframe_start > 35.0`)** or suffered multi-ingredient contamination (`closest_other_keyframe_l1_start < l1_vs_keyframe_start`):
  - **Part 1**:
    - `clip_01` (`l1_start=57.73`, `max_internal_jump_l1=51.65` at `9.0s`): Contaminated by `clip_04` (`closest_other_keyframe_l1_start=35.99`).
    - `clip_02` (`max_internal_jump_l1=42.21` at `0.5s`): Hard internal cut at `0.5s`; shows Lemonella & Banana standing politely with champagne instead of kissing.
    - `clip_03` (`l1_start=59.10`): Keyframe dropped -> T2V hallucination.
    - `clip_06` (`l1_start=48.89`): Keyframe dropped -> T2V hallucinated a **daytime cartoon eggplant in a Renaissance prince outfit** on a balcony!
  - **Part 2**:
    - `clip_04` (`l1_start=60.20`): Keyframe dropped -> T2V hallucination.
    - `clip_05` (`l1_start=67.86`, `closest_other_keyframe=clip_06` at `12.03`, `max_internal_jump_l1=62.74` at `0.5s`): Leftover `clip_06` keyframe chip in composer + Banana Man rendered with a **realistic human man's face inside a banana costume**!
  - **Part 3**:
    - In `flow_6clip_master_part3.jpg` (rendered at 09:52 AM before `raw_unsubbed` was updated, and still showing `clip_04` `l1_start=45.36` in `parts1_to_5_clips_probe.json`):
      - The Instagram caption is all about **Mafia Boss Shadow Fig (`سایه انجیر سیاه`)**.
      - Yet in `flow_6clip_master_part3.jpg`, at `34.84s` (`clip_04`) and `54.84s` (`clip_06`), **Shadow Fig is completely missing**—replaced by an Eggplant in a suit speaking Shadow Fig's mafia dialogue! And at `44.84s` (`clip_05`), Lemonella turns into a **human blonde woman**!
  - **Part 4**:
    - `clip_03` (`flow_6clip_master_part4.jpg` at `24.88s`): Countess Strawberry (`کنتس توت‌فرنگی`) turns into a **human brunette woman** sitting on a sofa!
    - `clip_04` (`l1_start=43.33`, `max_internal_jump_l1=38.16` at `1.5s`) and `clip_06` (`l1_start=56.56`, `max_internal_jump_l1=47.28` at `9.0s`): Dropped keyframes & internal jump cuts; zero attackers shown during the mansion storm scene.
  - **Part 5**:
    - `clip_01` (`l1_start=54.69`), `clip_02` (`l1_start=38.66`), `clip_03` (`l1_start=48.96`), `clip_06` (`l1_start=72.32`): **4 out of 6 clips dropped their keyframes!**
    - In `flow_6clip_master_part5.jpg`, at `34.80s` (`clip_04`), Duke Eggplant is randomly **shirtless like a bodybuilder**, and at `52.36s` (`clip_06`), Lemonella turns into a **human girl** in a hospital bed with a green pear doctor (`دکتر گلابی` in caption vs `دکتر هلو` in manifest) and no triplets ultrasound screen!

### Bug 3: `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py` Had Zero Visual Continuity, Keyframe Lock, Story-Beat Coverage, or Dead-Air Pacing Gates
- Previously, `farsi_flow_workflow.py` only validated audio loudness (`-14 LUFS`), speech RMS trim boundaries, and subtitle pill widths.
- Even when 4 out of 6 clips in an episode had `l1_vs_keyframe_start > 45.0` (wrong scene/character), `max_internal_jump_l1 > 40.0` (hard mid-clip cut), or 7 seconds of frozen dead air, `qa_report.json` reported `"all_passed": true`!

### Bug 4: Missing Hybrid Source-Bridge & Audio-Replacement Modes in `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py`
- When localizing an existing high-production 3D animated episode (`14_2026-10-19_DeqOJQTqU1S.mp4` .. `10_2026-10-20_DeugJXCPEYM.mp4`):
  - Action/bridge shots (the phone text message screen, the kiss on the yellow sports car, the DNA letter reveal, the rainy mansion exterior, the triplets ultrasound screen) **do not require lip-sync generation in Google Flow**—the original source video already has 100% consistent 3D characters, props, and fast 3-second cuts!
  - Furthermore, when Google Flow generates native Farsi dialogue clips (`partX/flow_clips/clip_01..06.mp4`), the pipeline needs two clean architectural modes in addition to pure clip-concatenation:
    1. **Hybrid Intercut Mode (`segment_type: "flow_clip"` + `"source_bridge"`)**: Uses validated Google Flow clips ONLY for tight lip-synced dialogue windows (`3–5s`, trimmed tightly around `first_speech_sec .. last_speech_sec` so there is zero 7-second dead air!) and intercuts clean inpainted bridge segments (`source_bridge`) directly from the original 3D episode MP4 for visual cause-and-effect shots (phone screen, kiss, DNA envelope, Shadow Fig in the rain, ultrasound triplets screen), OR falls back to the inpainted original video segment whenever a Google Flow clip fails the visual continuity gate (`KEYFRAME_MISMATCH` / `HUMAN_FACE_HALLUCINATION`)!
    2. **Original-Video + Flow-Native-Audio Track Mode (`video_source_mode: "original_inpainted_with_flow_audio"`)**: Keeps 100% of the original 16–20 scene 3D video footage (with English subtitles cleanly inpainted in the subtitle band and covered by the Farsi subtitle pill), extracts the natural Google Flow native Farsi voice lines from `flow_clips/clip_01..06.mp4`, places them onto the episode timeline over the ducked stereo side-channel BGM bed (`pan=stereo|c0=0.55*c0-0.35*c1|c1=0.55*c1-0.35*c0`), and renders the Farsi RTL subtitles!

   - **6-Stage Pipeline Step Trace (`step_trace` in `qa_report.json` and `trace_<episode_id>.json`)**:
     - `step_1_story_beat_and_character_lock`: Checks that every clip has `keyframe`, `expected_speakers`, and `story_beat` metadata; detects `CHARACTER_KEYFRAME_COLLISION` (two incompatible characters sharing the same keyframe SHA-256); checks speaker-to-caption consistency (e.g., `PEACH` vs `دکتر گلابی`); and flags `DEAD_AIR_PACING` if a static talking clip's trimmed duration exceeds active speech duration (`last_speech_sec - first_speech_sec`) by more than `3.5s`.
     - `step_2_keyframe_preflight`: Verifies untouched 9:16 keyframe dimensions and SHA-256.
     - `step_3_visual_continuity_audit`: Implements `analyze_clip_visuals(mp4_path, keyframe_path=..., all_keyframes=..., sample_fps=2.0)` and `evaluate_probe_entry(probe_entry, ...)` (so `trace-audit` works both on live MP4 files and on `docs/series2-meaning-and-pipeline-solution/parts1_to_5_clips_probe.json` in headless CI):
       - Flags `KEYFRAME_MISMATCH` when `l1_vs_keyframe_start > 32.0` AND `l1_vs_keyframe_mean > 32.0` (detecting dropped keyframes / T2V hallucinations across Parts 1–5).
       - Flags `MULTI_INGREDIENT_CONTAMINATION` when `closest_other_keyframe_l1_start + 5.0 < l1_vs_keyframe_start` (detecting leftover composer chips like Part 1 `clip_01` and Part 2 `clip_05`).
       - Flags `INTERNAL_SCENE_CUT` when `max_internal_jump_l1 > 32.0` (detecting mid-clip jump cuts like Part 1 `clip_01`/`clip_02`, Part 2 `clip_05`, Part 4 `clip_04`/`clip_06`), and computes `suggested_visual_trim_in` / `suggested_visual_trim_out`.
     - `step_4_acoustic_speech_and_pacing`: Logs `first_speech_sec`, `last_speech_sec`, `speech_duration_sec`, `dead_air_sec`, and supports `"tight_speech_trim": true` (which automatically trims static talking-head clips to `[max(0, first_speech_sec - pre_handle), min(dur, last_speech_sec + post_handle)]` so a 3.5-second line never sits in 6.5 seconds of awkward silence!).
     - `step_5_hybrid_bridge_and_fallback_resolution`:
       - Supports `source_video` at the episode level and `source_span: [start_sec, end_sec]` on each clip (plus optional `bridge_clips` / `fallback_to_source_on_visual_fail: true`).
       - When `fallback_to_source_on_visual_fail: true` (or `mode: "hybrid_source_and_flow"`), if a Google Flow clip fails `step_3_visual_continuity_audit` (`KEYFRAME_MISMATCH`, `MULTI_INGREDIENT_CONTAMINATION`, or `INTERNAL_SCENE_CUT`), the workflow can automatically replace the corrupted video stream of that clip with the clean subtitle-inpainted `source_span` from `source_video` while keeping the natural Google Flow native Farsi audio from `clip_XX.mp4`!
