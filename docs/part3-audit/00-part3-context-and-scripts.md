## 2. How the Scripts Were Used to Produce Part 3 (Step-by-Step Execution Log)

To produce **Series 4 (Fruits of Power) — Part 3 (`Master_FruitsOfPower_Part3_Flow_Native_Farsi.mp4`)**, three scripts in this repository were executed in sequence:

1. **Step A — Automated Google Flow Batch Submission (`scripts/submit_flow_batch.mjs`)**:
   - Connects to two logged-in Chrome profiles running Google Flow (`https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow/project/...`) via `ProfileBridge` MCP (`browser_observe_page`, `browser_click`, `browser_press_key`, `browser_type`).
   - Copies a 9:16 keyframe image (`KF_01.jpg`..`KF_06.jpg`) to the Windows clipboard via PowerShell `System.Windows.Forms.Clipboard::SetImage`, clicks the Google Flow prompt box (`What do you want to create?` / `چه چیزی می‌خواهید بسازید؟`), presses `Ctrl+V` to upload the image as an **Ingredient (`Ingredient` / `عنصر`)**, types the scene prompt with native Persian (Farsi) dialogue, and clicks `Start generation` (`شروع تولید`).
2. **Step B — Automated Google Flow Video Downloader (`scripts/download_flow_clips.mjs`)**:
   - Switches to the `videocam Videos` tab in Batch/Row view, deletes failed generation tiles (`Delete` / `حذف`), searches for completed video rows matching `--match <title_substring>`, clicks `Download` (`بارگیری`), waits for the new `.mp4` in `C:\Users\LifeCycle\Downloads`, and copies it to `part3\flow_clips\raw_unsubbed\clip_01.mp4`..`clip_06.mp4`.
3. **Step C — Episode Assembly, Speech-Safe Trimming & RTL Subtitle Burning (`pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py`)**:
   - Invoked via:
     ```powershell
     python pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py build --manifest pipeline/manifests/fruits_of_power_part3.json --force
     ```
   - Normalizes each clip (`clip_01.mp4`..`clip_06.mp4`) to `720x1280` @ `24fps` (`loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11`), runs `analyze_clip_speech()` (16kHz PCM RMS energy speech boundary check), burns speaker-colored Persian RTL subtitles (`Vazirmatn-Bold.ttf`) + top episode badge (`قسمت سوم: سایه انجیر سیاه`), concatenates into `Base_FruitsOfPower_Part3_Flow_Native_Farsi.mp4`, appends Duke Eggplant's Kelidbox CTA (`cta_kelidbox_s2.mp4`), and writes `Master_FruitsOfPower_Part3_Flow_Native_Farsi.mp4`, `qa_FruitsOfPower_Part3_contact.jpg`, and `qa_report.json`.

## 4. The 4 Root-Cause Pipeline Deficiencies That Must Be Fixed

1. **Deficiency 1 — No Pre-Flight Keyframe Sanitizer (`sanitize-keyframes`) in `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py`**:
   - Why did `part3/keyframes/KF_02.jpg`, `KF_04.jpg`, and `KF_06.jpg` trigger Google Flow's `"interests of third-party content providers"` filter when uploaded raw?
     - `KF_02.jpg`: Banana Man's bare human eyes are visible on his banana head (resembling Fortnite's trademarked **Peely**; whenever Banana Man wears dark sunglasses over his eyes, Google Flow passes 100%), AND Duke Eggplant is visible on the far-right edge (`x > 82%`) of the frame.
     - `KF_04.jpg`: Contains a glowing orange lit cigar tip + smoke (`x=40..62%, y=52..66%`) and a Rolls-Royce car grille in the bottom-right corner (`x=80..100%, y=86..100%`), plus lacks a distinct gold monocle/villain accent.
     - `KF_05.jpg`: Deep cleavage on Lemonella's gold dress (`x=32..60%, y=62..78%`) can trigger safety/fallback behavior.
     - `KF_06.jpg`: Contains a glowing lit cigar protruding from Shadow Fig's mouth (`x=46..63%, y=31..39%`) and smoke plume (`x=24..64%, y=0..28%`).
   - Instead of substituting keyframes from other episodes/characters (`part1/KF_03.jpg` or `part2/KF_06.jpg`), `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py` MUST provide a deterministic **`sanitize-keyframes`** subcommand (and helper functions) that modifies ONLY the offending local regions in `KF_01..06.jpg` while preserving 100% of each character's true head silhouette and scene composition!

2. **Deficiency 2 — Multi-Ingredient Contamination & Missing Single-Ingredient Lock in `scripts/submit_flow_batch.mjs`**:
   - In `scripts/submit_flow_batch.mjs`, clicking `Clear prompt` once did not clear multiple leftover `Ingredient` (`عنصر`) chips when a previous upload timed out or retried `Ctrl+V`.
   - `scripts/submit_flow_batch.mjs` MUST:
     a) Before pasting, count all `Ingredient` / `عنصر` chips and `Clear prompt` / `پاک کردن پیام‌واره` buttons in the composer and clear/remove them until `ingredientCount === 0`.
     b) After pasting `Ctrl+V`, verify that **exact `ingredientCount === 1`** (if `> 1`, clear and re-paste once).
     c) Never substitute `part1/KF_03.jpg` for Shadow Fig (`FIG`) or `part2/KF_06.jpg` for Part 3 Clip 2; point `BATCHES.fop_part3` to the sanitized Part 3 keyframes (`keyframes_sanitized/KF_01..06.jpg`).
     d) Write a JSONL trace record (`flow_submission_trace.jsonl`) for every submitted clip with `clip`, `kf_path`, `kf_sha256`, `ingredient_count_before`, `ingredient_count_after`, `prompt_sha256`, and `status`.

3. **Deficiency 3 — Missing Post-Generation Visual Continuity & Mid-Clip Scene-Cut Gate (`analyze_clip_visuals`) in `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py`**:
   - `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py` previously only checked audio/video stream metadata (`ffprobe`) and acoustic speech RMS (`analyze_clip_speech`), reporting `overall_automatic: "pass"` even when `clip_02`, `clip_04`, `clip_05`, and `clip_06` had massive keyframe mismatches (`L1 > 37`) and mid-clip hard cuts (`internal_jump_l1 > 40`)!
   - `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py` MUST implement **`analyze_clip_visuals(mp4_path, keyframe_path=None, sample_fps=2.0)`** (using `ffmpeg` rawvideo RGB24 pipe -> NumPy/Pillow, no OpenCV required) that:
     a) Samples frames across the clip (or within `[trim_in, trim_out]`), computing:
        - `l1_vs_keyframe_mean`, `l1_vs_keyframe_start` (at `trim_in + 0.5s`), `l1_vs_keyframe_mid`, `l1_vs_keyframe_end` against the clip's reference `keyframe` (if provided in the manifest or inferred from `keyframes/KF_XX.jpg`).
        - Adjacent-sample frame-to-frame L1 deltas (`max_internal_jump_l1`, `max_internal_jump_time_sec`, and list of `detected_scene_cuts` where step-delta exceeds threshold, default `32.0`).
     b) Supports an automatic **Ingredient-Prefix Cut Trimmer / Suggestion (`suggest_visual_trim`)**: if a hard cut is detected in the first `0.5..2.8s` (where Google Flow showed the static ingredient keyframe before cutting to the animated shot) or at the tail, reports `suggested_visual_trim_in` / `suggested_visual_trim_out`.
     c) Records a `visual_continuity` gate (`pass` / `warn` / `fail`) on every clip in `qa_report.json` and fails `overall_automatic` when `visual_gate_mode == "strict"` and a clip exceeds `max_keyframe_l1` (default `35.0`) or `max_scene_jump_l1` (default `32.0`)!

4. **Deficiency 4 — Missing Step-by-Step Pipeline Trace (`step_trace` & `trace-audit` Subcommand) in `pipeline/farsi_flow_workflow.py`**:
   - Add a structured `step_trace` array to `qa_report.json` (and write `<out_dir>/trace_<episode_id>.json` + new CLI subcommand `trace-audit`) that records every step of the pipeline for each clip:
     - `step_1_manifest_character_lock`: Checks speaker IDs, character-to-keyframe uniqueness (flags an error if two incompatible speakers like `DUKE` and `FIG` use the same keyframe SHA-256), and dialogue-to-subtitle match.
     - `step_2_keyframe_preflight`: Verifies keyframe 9:16 aspect ratio, file hash, and whether sanitization was applied.
     - `step_3_visual_continuity_audit`: Logs `l1_vs_keyframe_mean`, `max_internal_jump_l1`, `detected_scene_cuts`, and specifically flags which clips in an episode fail visual consistency (e.g., on Part 3's raw probe data, must flag `clip_02`, `clip_04`, `clip_05`, `clip_06` while passing `clip_01` and `clip_03`).
     - `step_4_acoustic_speech_audit`: Logs `first_speech_sec`, `last_speech_sec`, `trim_in`, `trim_out`, `cuts_speech`.
     - `step_5_subtitle_shaping_and_bounds`: Logs RTL shaping, pill width fit, and cue bounds.
     - `step_6_mux_and_master_verification`: Logs per-clip A/V stream checks, `audio_contiguous`, Base/Master durations, and CTA idempotency.
